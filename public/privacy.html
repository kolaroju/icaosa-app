Privacy policy

ICAOSA app · Last updated 20 November 2026

The ICAOSA app is the members' app of the Ibadan City Academy Old Students Association ("ICAOSA"). It is run for ICAOSA by Gamich Consults. This page explains what the app collects and why. Questions: CONTACT_EMAIL.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Member profile: full name, graduating set, birthday (day and month only, never the year), and, if you add them, WhatsApp number, city and occupation.
  • What you post: news, invites, events, RSVPs and appeals you create.
  • Payments: amount, date, purpose and status of dues and donations. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's secure page and are never sent to or stored by ICAOSA.
  • Device notification token (Android app), so we can send the notifications you switch on.

How we use it

To run the association: show your set's feed, events and member directory; send reminders and notifications; record dues and donations; and let set treasurers see their set's payments. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • Association members can see your name, set, city, occupation, birthday (day and month) and WhatsApp number in the directory.
  • Your set's excos and the association admins can see payment records for your set.
  • Service providers process data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in), Paystack (payments), Brevo (sign-in emails), Expo and Google Firebase (notifications and app delivery).

Keeping and deleting your data

You can edit your profile at any time in the app. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account), or see how to request deletion. Deleting removes your profile, posts, RSVPs and devices. Payment records are kept for the association's accounts but are no longer linked to you.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Write to CONTACT_EMAIL.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and tell members in the app.
