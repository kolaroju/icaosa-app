Delete your ICAOSA account

You can delete your ICAOSA account and personal data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open the ICAOSA app and sign in.
  2. Tap your initials at the top right to open Your profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Without the app

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my ICAOSA account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

Your sign-in account, member profile (name, set, birthday, phone, city, occupation), your posts, events, RSVPs and notification devices.

What is kept

Records of dues and donations you paid are kept for the association's financial records, as the law requires, but are no longer linked to your name or account.
