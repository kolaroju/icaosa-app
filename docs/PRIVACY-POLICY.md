# ICAOSA app privacy policy

*Last updated: [DATE]. Replace the text in square brackets before publishing, and have the association's legal adviser review it.*

The ICAOSA app is run by the Ibadan City Academy Old Students Association ("ICAOSA", "we"), [registered address]. This policy explains what the app collects and why. Contact: [email address].

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address, used to sign you in.
- **Your member profile:** full name, graduating set, birthday (day and month only, never the year), and, if you choose to add them, WhatsApp number, city and occupation.
- **What you post:** news, invites, events, RSVPs and appeals you create.
- **Payments:** the amount, date, purpose and status of dues and donations you make or record. Card and bank details are entered on Paystack's secure page and are never sent to or stored by ICAOSA.
- **Device notification token:** so we can send you notifications you have switched on.

## How we use it

To run the association: show your set's feed, events and directory; send reminders and notifications; record dues and donations; and let set treasurers confirm payments. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

## Who can see it

- **Members of the association** can see your name, set, city, occupation, birthday (day and month) and WhatsApp number in the directory.
- **Your set's excos and the association admins** can see your payment records for your set.
- **Service providers** process data for us: Supabase (database and sign-in), Paystack (payments), Expo, Google Firebase and Apple (push notifications), and our email provider (sign-in codes).

## Keeping and deleting your data

You can edit your profile at any time. You can delete your account in the app (**Profile → Delete my account**). This removes your profile, posts, RSVPs and devices. Payment records are kept for the association's accounts but are no longer linked to you.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. Write to [email address].

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell members in the app.
